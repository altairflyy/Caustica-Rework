## Erratum: characterization helper EOL handling

AER-004 initially observed 9 failures. Subsequent analysis showed that five
of those observations were generated by the CRLF-sensitive `slice()` helper
in `RtParallaxShaderRegressionTest`, not by renderer behavior. The helper now
normalizes CRLF/CR to LF before slicing shader text.

The canonical baseline is therefore the exact four genuine failures:

- `sideWallsReplaceTheMappedNormalOnBothHitPaths`
- `blockSpritesTileWhileEntityAtlasesStopAtTheirIsland`
- `continuationOriginsStayOffTheRestPlaneMesh`
- `animatedWaterIntersectsTheHeightFieldAlongTheViewRay`

The historical nine-failure evidence is retained above. No production Java,
shader, or rendering behavior was modified.
